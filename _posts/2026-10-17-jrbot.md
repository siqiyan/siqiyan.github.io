This small two-wheels robot platform is built for indoor experiement based on
Robot Operating System (ROS). It has standard ROS message support for
controling its motion and receive wheel odometry. I also added LiDAR (model:
rplidar c1) and USB camera for navigation and vision tasks. Its motor control
is implemented on an Arduino Nano with customized firmware, and the high level
navigation and vision tasks are implemented on NVIDIA Jetson Nano. The battery is
5200mAh at 8.4v which is sufficient for its tasks. The maximum speed is around
0.15m/s.

![JRBot Front](/assets/img/jrbot_front.jpg)

![JRBot Side](/assets/img/jrbot_back.jpg)


## Assembly

The robot consists of three levels. The first level is for the motor
controller, Arduino, battery, voltage converter (for Nvidia Jetson Nano),
motors and all-directional wheels.

![JRBot L1](/assets/img/jrbot_l1.jpg)

The second level is for LiDAR (RpLidar C1).

![JRBot L2](/assets/img/jrbot_l2.jpg)

The third level is for Nvidia Jetson Nano and USB camera. All three level are
connected by M3 copper pillars and bolts.

![JRBot L3](/assets/img/jrbot_l3.jpg)
